I'm an Applied Scientist at [The Trade Desk](https://www.thetradedesk.com/), where I work on conversion modeling: turning sparse and delayed user signals into features and statistical learning models that estimate the probability of a conversion. These estimates feed measurement and bidding decisions across the platform, so much of my work is about keeping them accurate and stable at scale.

I received my Ph.D. in Data Science from [New York University](https://cds.nyu.edu/) in May 2026, advised by [Prof. Shuyang Ling](https://cims.nyu.edu/~sling/index.html) at [NYU Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/) and the [Center for Data Science](https://cds.nyu.edu/). Before that, I received a bachelor's degree in Mathematics from [New York University Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/).



My research centers on **reliable estimation and uncertainty quantification**, from statistical theory to large-scale applications:

- **High-dimensional statistics and spectral methods:** entrywise estimation, uncertainty quantification, group synchronization, rank aggregation
- **Trustworthy machine learning:** calibration, domain adaptation under distribution shift, and the reliability of LLM, RAG and agent systems
- **Applied statistical learning:** conversion modeling from sparse and delayed signals, feature learning, and measurement in online advertising
