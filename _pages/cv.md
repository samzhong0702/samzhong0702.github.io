## Research Highlights

- **Perturbation theory on matrix manifolds (Ph.D. dissertation).** A Riemannian Newton framework for non-asymptotic perturbation analysis of estimators on matrix manifolds. It gives distribution-free ℓ₂-type guarantees under deterministic perturbations and, under probabilistic noise, blockwise distributional guarantees and valid confidence regions, with applications to spectral ranking, eigenvector perturbation (sharpening the Davis–Kahan theorem) and orthogonal group synchronization. An extension to singular vector perturbation and sub-Gaussian noise is in preparation.
- **Uncertainty quantification for group synchronization.** Distributional theory for the spectral estimator and MLE in orthogonal group synchronization, enabling confidence intervals and hypothesis tests for applications such as cryo-EM, robotics and computer vision (*IEEE Transactions on Information Theory*, 2026).
- **Spectral ranking.** Near-optimal sample complexity guarantees for rank aggregation from noisy pairwise comparisons (*Information and Inference*, 2024).
- **Domain adaptation.** Approximately shared, rather than strictly invariant, features provably support generalization under distribution shift (AISTATS 2025).
