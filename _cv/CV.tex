\section*{Research Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Perturbation theory on matrix manifolds (Ph.D.\ dissertation).} A Riemannian Newton framework for non-asymptotic perturbation analysis of estimators on matrix manifolds: distribution-free $\ell_2$-type guarantees under deterministic perturbations and, under probabilistic noise, blockwise distributional guarantees and valid confidence regions. Applications to spectral ranking, eigenvector perturbation (sharpening the Davis--Kahan theorem) and orthogonal group synchronization; an extension to singular vector perturbation and sub-Gaussian noise is in preparation.
  \item \textbf{Uncertainty quantification for group synchronization.} Distributional theory for the spectral estimator and MLE in orthogonal group synchronization, enabling confidence intervals and hypothesis tests for applications such as cryo-EM, robotics and computer vision (\textit{IEEE Trans.\ Inf.\ Theory}, 2026).
  \item \textbf{Spectral ranking.} Near-optimal sample complexity guarantees for rank aggregation from noisy pairwise comparisons (\textit{Information and Inference}, 2024).
  \item \textbf{Domain adaptation.} Approximately shared, rather than strictly invariant, features provably support generalization under distribution shift (AISTATS 2025).
\end{itemize}
